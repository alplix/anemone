Carroll J. Glynn

Biography

Carroll J. Glynn is a communication researcher who works on the social-psychological aspects of public opinion and social norms. Glynn earned a doctorate at the University of Wisconsin, worked for a long time as a professor at the Ohio State University School of Communication, ran the school, and is now an emeritus professor.

Glynn is one of the authors of Public Opinion, a university textbook on public opinion prepared with several co-authors; the book has gone through several editions.

In the spiral of silence literature, Glynn is best known for the meta-analysis published with Andrew Hayes and James Shanahan in Public Opinion Quarterly in 1997: by bringing together earlier survey studies, the work evaluated a testable premise of the spiral, that is, the relationship between perceived support and willingness to speak.
