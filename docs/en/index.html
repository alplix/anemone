How it works

  • Bite-sized cards. Every lesson is a sequence of one-minute cards, each followed by a small question.
  • Go as deep as you like. Core cards are the path; optional deep-dive cards add the finest detail.
  • Remember it. Spaced review boxes and a mistakes notebook bring back what you forget.
  • Exams. Unit exams, mixed mock exams, timed or untimed, with an analysis by topic.
  • Your progress is not lost. Sign in and your progress, statistics and review boxes are stored in the database and follow you to every device. As a guest they stay in this browser.
  • Built for everyone. No sound, keyboard and screen-reader friendly, seven display themes including e-ink paper mode.
