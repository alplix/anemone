## (b) Judgement calls

1. **Terminology.** "suskunluk sarmalı" everywhere (owner rule). Polat's unit title says "Sihirli Mermi ve Sessizlik Sarmalı" and his key-concepts list "Sessizlik Kuramı" (section title: "Suskunluk Sarmalı Kuramı"); Yaylagül: "Suskunluk Sarmalı". These alternatives appear ONLY in the `exam` section ("kitapta ... adıyla geçer") and, as the single word "sessizlik" (never the phrase "sessizlik sarmalı"), in one quiz question about alternative names (`quiz-alt-names`). If the owner prefers zero occurrences, remove that question or reword it.
2. **Choice of Turkish terms:** "fikir iklimi" (Polat's term; alternatives listed), "sert çekirdek" (Polat + Aktaş 2024), "dışlanma korkusu" (Polat, Yaylagül, Kutlu, Özer & Yıldıran Önk, Kehya), "yarı istatistiksel algı" and "sosyal derimiz" flagged as unsettled with the English term in parentheses at first mention.
3. **Nazi-era journalism** is included (not omitted) because two independent, reputable sources (Wilke, a sympathetic insider; Wendelin/BLexKom, more critical) both address it. Bio (person file) contains only facts both agree on. The lesson card reports each source's own emphasis with attribution and separates the empirical question (does the theory hold?) from the biographical one; it passes no verdict.
4. **`turkey` section included** because two Turkish studies could be read in full and cross-checked (Kutlu 2019 survey; Özer & Yıldıran Önk 2021 press analysis), plus the 1998 translation and both course books. The Karaca card contains one clearly labelled critical-thinking paragraph of my own (legal restrictions as an alternative mechanism) that the authors partly acknowledge ("baskı rejimi ile örtüşüyor").
5. **1965 as the puzzle, but not the only origin.** Card `hook` uses 1965 (AUZEF, Petersen, Özer & Yıldıran Önk agree); a deep card explains that Wilke and Mader also point to 1969/1972/1976 and that Griffin's textbook opens with the 1980 US election (a trap for AUZEF students).
6. **Critique fairness.** Critics (Griffin's three points, Scheufele & Moy) and defenders' replies are both given; the verdict ("kısmen desteklenen, koşullu") is labelled as my own synthesis, based on the two meta-analyses and Thurre et al.'s remark that all meta-analysed studies were micro-level.
7. **Hypothetical material** (group-chat lab, campus case, dorm example in the concept file) is explicitly labelled "Varsayımsal örnek / düşünce deneyi"; no invented anecdote is presented as fact.
8. **Asch as a background thread:** core card (how Noelle-Neumann used him, per Griffin; Asch's own emphasis on independence from the 1956 text) + deep card with monograph numbers.
9. **Sources by independence.** noelle-neumann.de is run by colleagues, Petersen works at Allensbach, Wilke is her successor: they are used, attributed, and cross-checked against catalogues (DNB, Open Library) or independent sources (Wendelin, epd, Griffin).
10. **Word counts.** Turkish cards count fewer words than English ones; all core cards are 149-192 words (excluding reference markers), deep cards 159-233.
11. **Glynn** kept minimal (two sources: OSU profile, publisher blurb of the textbook).
12. Habermas is named in plain text (no `[[person:]]` link; he is not on the roster); link goes to `[[lesson:public-sphere]]`.
13. Person files: Noelle-Neumann born 1916 / died 2010 (Wilke, epd, Wendelin); Asch born 1907 / died 1996 (Penn Almanac, EBSCO, Daily Pennsylvanian). Only years; places only where at least two sources agree (Berlin; Warsaw; Haverford).
