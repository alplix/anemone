# Translation notes: `research-methods` (English, 2026-09-20)

Files: `content/i18n/en/courses/mass-communication-theories/lessons/research-methods.json`; concepts `survey-research sampling experiment-method content-analysis qualitative-research validity reliability effect-size correlation-causation meta-analysis replication-crisis`. No people entries were created. Status `machine` on all.

## Adaptations

- **Turkish terms kept in the `turkey` and `exam` cards.** These two cards discuss the Turkish names of the same concepts (güvenirlik/güvenilirlik, geçerlik/geçerlilik, içerik analizi/içerik çözümlemesi), so the Turkish words are kept (with the English term beside them). They are the only intentional Turkish left in the English lesson, apart from surnames (Ünal) and the Turkish `altTerms` in the concept files, as in existing concept files.
- **Lincoln and Guba's four criteria.** The Turkish text gives the Turkish word with the English term in brackets (inandırıcılık (credibility), and so on); the English text gives the English terms only.
- **"Kura" (lottery).** The Turkish text uses "kura" for random assignment ("kurayla atama"); English uses "lottery" or "random assignment" depending on register.
- **"Sarmal".** The Turkish deep card on reinforcing spirals adds a line telling the reader not to confuse it with "suskunluk sarmalı"; English says "spiral of silence".
- **"Hayal et:"** is rendered "Imagine ..." throughout; "bizim yorumumuz / bizim örneğimiz / bizim çıkarımımız" as "our interpretation / our example / our inference".
- **Numbers.** Turkish thousands separators (689.003, 6.000) become commas (689,003; 6,000); decimal commas (2,4 milyon, 0,02) become points (2.4 million, 0.02). Percentages written "yüzde 54" become "54 percent".
- **Quiz option order.** Four multiple-choice questions had their options reordered in the Turkish source (to vary the position of the right answer); the English options follow the same final order and the answer indexes are identical.
- **Payne Fund titles.** English film and study titles are kept in the original English (*The Jazz Singer*, *Michael Strogoff*, *Four Sons*, *The Emden*).
- Nothing was added to or removed from the Turkish text except where noted above.

## Doubts

- "Focus group discussion" and "focused interview" (odak grup görüşmesi / odaklı görüşme) are kept as two separate terms because the sources (Merton & Kendall 1946; Lee 2010) treat them as related but not identical.
- The phrase "manifest content" for Berelson's definition is the standard English term; the Turkish "görünen (yüzeydeki) içerik" is our rendering.
