## (a) Could not verify, therefore left out (or explicitly hedged)

Study numbers and quotations
- **Exact percentages of the 1976 “TV answer” questions.** Sources disagree: Özer 2005 says the answer options were “yüzde bir / onda bir” (1 in 100 / 1 in 10); Morgan in the 2010 MEF film says “1 in 100 or 1 in 50”; Yaylagül 2006 gives a different law-enforcement example (12% of TV characters vs 1% of the workforce). The Gerbner & Gross 1976 PDF I found (MIT course reading) is a scan without a text layer and I had no OCR. The lesson therefore names the question type but gives **no percentages** and says so on the card `evidence-profile`.
- **Hour threshold for “heavy viewer”.** “4+ hours/day” appears in web summaries (Wikipedia, İletişim Ansiklopedisi) but I did not see it in a primary text. Verified only: Erdoğan 1998 (az/orta/çok), Özer 2005 (its own study: ≤2 h “az”, >2 h “çok”). The lesson says the threshold varies and that “dört saat” may appear in some books.
- **Mean World Index** (the three-item index): only web-teaching-resource snippets; not used.
- **First-order / second-order** is used with Potter 1991 (abstract) and Yaylagül 2006 (“ilk düzey / ikincil düzey etkiler”). I did not open Hawkins & Pingree 1982 (the usual origin) and do not attribute the distinction to them.
- **Gerbner et al. 1980 (mainstreaming article) full text**: paywalled. Its findings (mainstreaming among income groups, ceiling effect, resonance among big-city residents) are described only via Özer 2005’s Turkish account (which cites it as 1980a with page numbers) and Morgan 2008; both stated in the cards as “Özer’in aktardığına göre”.
- **Fletcher ballad saying**: only paraphrased (23 words in the original, above the 15-word limit).
- **Streaming (Netflix etc.)**: I found no verified cultivation study specific to streaming/VOD; the `today` card says so and confines itself to (i) 2021 TV meta-analysis, (ii) 2023 social media meta-analysis, (iii) Williams 2006, (iv) Tsay-Vogel et al. 2018, (v) clearly labelled reasoning. No personalised-feed cultivation study was verified (an arXiv 2026 preprint on “algorithmic cultivation” turned up in a search; not opened, not used).
- **Newcomb 1978, Wober 1978 (British replication), Gerbner’s reply to Newcomb, Hirsch’s POQ note “On Hughes’ contribution”**: exist in Morgan’s Cultural Indicators bibliography; not opened, not cited. Content of Hirsch’s “nonviewer” anomaly not described (only the article’s abstract-level claims are used).
- **Ünür 2022 (Haliç Üniv. SBD 5(1), 119–161: Turkish cinema, violence and sexuality via Cultural Indicators)** and other Turkish DergiPark studies (Konya lise; Gaziantep women viewers): seen only in search results/summary; not verified and not cited.
- **Özer 2011 (Informatologia 44(3))**: cited in Morgan’s bibliography, and an identical abstract is the Crossref-verified Özer 2019 (Media, Culture and Public Relations 10(2)); only the 2019 record is cited. AVESİS lists a 2011 conference version (Society and Technology, Lovran); not used.
