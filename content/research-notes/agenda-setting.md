## (b) Judgement calls

1. **Turkey section kept.** Two Turkish studies whose full texts were read: Algül 2016 (front pages of six papers, 6 July–31 Aug 2015, 57 days) and Hazar & Sbouh 2020 (740 students; 386 + 354). Each claim rests on the study itself plus its Crossref/DergiPark record, not on two independent studies of the same finding. Flagged so the integrator can decide whether that meets the “sourced twice” bar. The Hazar & Sbouh PDF has a text layer with lost Turkish characters; every number used was cross-read against the English abstract or the table text.
2. **Single-source-but-attributed claims** (marked with the source in the sentence): newspaper-vs-television results of the Charlotte study, the 0/1, 0/1/2, 0/1/2…N models, the intrapersonal/interpersonal agenda distinction, the time-lag summary for 2–6 months, and the “five to seven issues” limit all come from McCombs 1976 (his own ERIC review, not independent). Funkhouser is backed by two abstracts plus McCombs 1976.
3. **“Correlation over a handful of issue categories.”** The paper’s Table 2 lists five main issues (foreign policy, law and order, fiscal policy, public welfare, civil rights) but I wrote only “birkaç ana konu kategorisi” because the paper does not say in one sentence how many data points the r values use.
4. **Survey question of 1968** is given as a close paraphrase (“kabaca”), not a translation; the original is > 15 words and appears only in the footnote.
5. **Kwak et al.:** “2009’un Twitter’ı” is my shorthand (July 2009 user count, four months of trends, paper published 2010).
6. **Hypothetical scenarios** are always labelled: restaurant waiter analogy, Ayşe/Berk/Ceren, the fictional inflation country, water bills vs foreign election, Kumsalköy.
7. **My own inferences** are labelled “benim yorumum / çıkarımım”: the “escape route” reading of the 1972 intro, the reconciliation of the two time-lag findings, and the algorithm paragraph.
8. **Quotes:** Cohen (9 words), Lang & Lang (8 words), Lippmann “too big, too complex, and too fleeting for direct acquaintance” (10), “By fictions I do not mean lies” (7), “To traverse the world men must have maps of the world” (10; person file). All exact, English, checked in the Gutenberg text / paper PDF.
9. **Lesson-meta roles:** only `primary` (original works and empirical studies) and `review` (Rogers & Dearing 1988, McCombs 1976, McCombs–Shaw–Weaver 2014, Scheufele & Tewksbury 2007, Weaver 2025). No textbook was opened, so none is listed.
10. **Timeline labels:** the contract has no place for event labels, so the tr lesson file carries an extra `timeline` object (id → label); the meta file lists `{year, id}`.
11. **`introducedBy`:** left empty for priming, salience, media/public agenda and gatekeeping (the persons Iyengar, Lewin, White have no person files here); `mccombs` for second-level and need-for-orientation (Weaver has no person file in this batch).
