## Doubts
- "Aracı gruplar" could also be "intermediary groups"; "intermediate groups" was chosen because it is Kornhauser's own phrase.
- "Vesayetçi" as "tutelary" is Reeve's wording; other translations of Tocqueville may say "protective".
- "Kitle" is rendered as "mass" in Kornhauser/Ortega contexts and as "crowd" where the source is Le Bon or Tarde; the glossary card explains it, but readers of the Turkish original see one word.
- Quotation marks: the English keeps curly quotes; no quotation over 15 words in either language.
